# Koejonnen — Game Rules

## Overview

- Koejonnen is played by 4 players in 2 teams of 2. You always play with the player sitting
  opposite you.
- Goal: be the first team to erase all lines of your "boomke" (scoreboard).

## Cards

- The game is played with 24 cards: 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace in all 4 suits.
- Trick rank order (high to low): Ace, King, Queen, Jack, 10, 9.
- Points: Ace = 4, King = 3, Queen = 2, Jack = 1, 10 and 9 = 0. Total per deal: 40 points.

## The boomke

- Each team starts with 13 lines. The first team to reach 0 lines wins the game.
- A "Koei" adds a line to your boomke. A Koei is traditionally drawn a bit crooked with a few
  hairs at the end, like a cow's tail.
- The line count can go above 13 through Koeis.

## Choosing the first dealer

- A player of team A picks up a packet of cards from the deck: at least 4 cards must be taken
  and at least 8 must remain. The bottom card of the packet is revealed.
- A player of team B does the same on the remaining deck, 4 must remain.
- Whoever shows the highest card chooses who deals first.
- On equal cards, both players draw again.

## Dealing

- The dealer's right neighbour cuts the deck. (Online: the deck is shuffled only before the first deal. After that, the trick piles are stacked and the cut is random.)
- The dealer deals clockwise in batches of 2 cards. Each player receives 6 cards.
- The dealer's last 2 cards stay separate: the second-to-last face down, the last one face
  up. The face-up card proposes the trump suit.
- The dealer may not look at their own cards during the bidding. Once trump is decided, the
  dealer picks up all their cards.

## Bidding

- The player left of the dealer speaks first: "play" or "pass". Then the next players
  clockwise.
- "Play" → the suit of the shown card becomes trump.
- If all 3 non-dealers pass → the dealer turns the second-to-last card face up.
  - Same suit as the first card: no new bidding round. The dealer immediately chooses play
    or pass.
  - Different suit: the 3 non-dealers bid again, in the same order, on this suit.
  - If all 3 pass again → the dealer chooses: play with either of the two shown suits as
    trump, or pass.
- If the deal is fully passed → the player left of the dealer becomes the new dealer and the
  stake on the first turned card doubles. Consecutive passed deals keep doubling
  (×2, ×4, ...). The stake on the second turned card never changes. Once a hand is played
  and scored, the stake returns to normal.

## Playing

- The player left of the dealer leads the first trick. There are 6 tricks per deal.
- If the partner of the player who called "play" leads the first trick, that player may
  ask for "Troefke": a request to open with a trump card. The partner is free to ignore
  the request.
- If a plain suit is led:
  - You must follow suit, or you may play a trump. Trump is never required but always
    allowed.
  - "No underbuy": if a trump is already in the trick and you can follow the led suit, you
    may only play a trump HIGHER than the highest trump in the trick. Throwing away a lower
    trump is not allowed.
  - If you cannot follow the led suit, you may play any card.
- If trump is led: you must play a trump if you have one, even a lower trump than those
  already played.
- The highest trump wins the trick. Without a trump, the highest card of the led suit wins.
  The winner of a trick leads the next one.

## Scoring

- Only Ace, King, Queen and Jack count (4, 3, 2, 1). There are 40 points per deal.
- The playing team is the team of the player who said "play" (or of the dealer if the dealer
  chose). The playing team wins the deal with more than 20 points. With 19 or fewer the
  defenders win. An exact 20-20 is a draw: nobody erases lines and no Koei is added, but
  the stake on the next deal's first turned card doubles (as after an all-passed deal).
- The winning team erases lines:
  - Deal played on the first turned card: 1 line × the stake.
  - Deal played on the second turned card: 2 lines (never multiplied).
- Kapot: if a team wins all 6 tricks, that team always erases 1 extra line — so 2 lines on
  the first card, 3 lines on the second card. Both teams can play kapot. The kapot bonus is
  never multiplied.
- If the defenders win, the playing team also adds 1 Koei — also when the defenders win by
  kapot.
- After scoring, the player left of the dealer becomes the new dealer and a new hand is
  dealt.

## End of the game

- The first team to erase all its lines wins the game.
